The patient reports difficulty moving the arm and experiences sharp pain when attempting to lift objects. Limited range of motion is observed in the shoulder. X-ray imaging shows no fracture but indicates soft tissue inflammation in the rotator cuff area.

Treatment plan includes rest, ice application to the affected shoulder, and anti-inflammatory medication. Follow-up appointment scheduled in one week to assess healing progress in the shoulder region.
